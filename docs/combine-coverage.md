# Combine coverage on the G100

Generated by `tools/conform/coverage.py` from conformance test t13, which draws
each combine state on the emulated G100 and on the retail Voodoo runtime.

- **exact**: matches the Voodoo within the conformance tolerance.
- **approx**: the G100 cannot express it; the nearest form is drawn and
  `MGA-DEGRADE` is logged once per state. On the G200 the specular add and
  native blending cover most of these.
